Card look
How each mail is drawn in cards view. "Theme" keeps what Thunderbird or your theme draws, for example flat rows.
Compact group headers
When a folder is grouped (View → Sort by → Grouped by Sort), each group header takes one slim line in cards view instead of a whole card.
All folders
Group every folder of every account by date, newest first, or switch them all back to conversations. New folders follow. Single folders can still be changed in View → Sort by.

Sender icons

Logos for senders your provider verified, initials for everyone else.

Verification per account

Your provider stamps every mail it checked. Detect reads that stamp from your recent mail. Only mail with a positive stamp gets a logo; fakes get initials with a red !.

Advanced
Brand logos (BIMI)
Official logos of big brands. The logo is downloaded from the brand's server, which can see your IP address.
DNS resolver for BIMI
Extra free-mail domains
Senders from these domains always get initials (gmail.com, gmx.de and others are built in).
Icons come from DuckDuckGo, which learns which domains mail you, not who. Senders' servers are never contacted.
